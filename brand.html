01 — Identity

Logo

The wordmark is used in the paper/white version on the dark navy field. Keep generous clear space (min. the height of the "A"), never recolor it, never place it on a busy image without a scrim, and never stretch it. The asterisk is the brand's secondary mark — used as bullets, section flourishes, and in loading/CTA moments.

✓ Do

Use on navy or a dark scrim · keep clear space · pair with the mark · let it breathe.

✕ Don't

Recolor · add effects/shadows · crowd it · place on low-contrast imagery · stretch or rotate.

04 — Spacing & Layout

Grid, spacing & radius

One fluid content container and one fluid gutter carry the whole site. Vertical rhythm comes from a large fluid section pad. Corners are a two-speed system: sharp/near-sharp for structural slabs and logo cells, soft rounded for cards, and full pills for buttons.

Layout tokens

--maxw 1400px content width
--pad clamp(20px, 5vw, 80px) gutter
.wrap centers content + applies pad
.rows section pad: clamp(70px, 13vh, 150px)
.cta pad: clamp(80px, 15vh, 200px)

Spacing scale & rules

52 pxsection head → content (.sec-head)
clamp(70px,13vh,150px)section vertical rhythm (.rows)
clamp(80px,15vh,200px)hero / CTA breathing room
  • Fluid over fixed. Use clamp() for anything that scales with the viewport — section padding, gutters, display type. Reserve fixed px for small non-scaling details (chip padding, hairline gaps).
  • Let it breathe. Vertical rhythm carries hierarchy more than borders do. One idea per band of space — don't crowd unrelated elements into the same gap.
  • Cap the measure. Body text lives at ~52–82ch (max-width on paragraphs). Never let body copy run full-bleed across the container.
  • Consistent inner padding per component. Cards ~24px, big panels clamp(26px,3vw,48px). Don't hand-tune padding instance by instance.
  • One container, one gutter. Align everything to .wrap (max 1400px) + --pad. Don't invent new arbitrary max-widths.

08 — Guardrails

Do & Don't

The fastest way to stay on-brand: keep orange rare, keep type in its lane, let space do the work, and make motion earn its place.

✓ Do — Accent

Exactly one orange focal point per view — a keyword (.o), a number, or one CTA.

✕ Don't — Accent

Multiple competing orange elements, orange body text, or large orange fills outside the deliberate invert panel.

✓ Do — Body text

Inter in --paper-dim on navy, line-height 1.6–1.7, measure capped at ~52–82ch.

✕ Don't — Body text

Pure white or orange body copy, tight line-height, or paragraphs running the full container width.

✓ Do — Type

Space Grotesk 700 caps for display, Inter for body. Exactly one <h1> per page, keyword-led.

✕ Don't — Type

Use heading tags purely for size (style with CSS instead), add a third typeface, or ship multiple H1s.

✓ Do — Imagery

Desaturate by default (grayscale + dim), warm to color on hover/active, and always add a bottom gradient scrim under text.

✕ Don't — Imagery

Full-brightness photos behind text with no scrim, or bright imagery that competes with the one orange accent.

✓ Do — Spacing

Generous, fluid section rhythm; let whitespace carry hierarchy. Consistent inner padding per component type.

✕ Don't — Spacing

Cram sections together and lean on borders to separate them, or hand-tune padding on every instance.

✓ Do — Motion

Purposeful, consistent motion with prefers-reduced-motion and touch fallbacks (.plain).

✕ Don't — Motion

Animate everything, block content behind long intros, or ship scroll effects with no reduced-motion path.

✓ Do — Logo

Paper wordmark on navy or a dark scrim, with clear space ≥ the height of the "A".

✕ Don't — Logo

Recolor, add shadows/effects, stretch/rotate, or place it on low-contrast imagery.
