What services does Plan A Technologies offer?

Plan A offers five core services — AI strategy & CTO advisory, data & AI foundations, AI agents & intelligent automation, software engineering & modernization, and 24/7 managed technical support — backed by more than 20 capabilities spanning custom software, cloud migration, DevOps, UI/UX and cybersecurity.

How do you engage with clients — project work or staffing?

Both. We take on fully-managed project work, provide dedicated talent (human engineers or AI agents) for individual roles or entire teams, and offer tech consulting and emergency support. You can scale the engagement up or down as your needs change.

Do you build with AI or traditional engineering?

Both, deliberately. We still do the hard custom development, integration and modernization work we've always done, and we use AI agents alongside expert engineers to deliver it faster and more efficiently.
