Gaming, Entertainment & Hospitality · Marketing

The Palms — Gamified Offers

The Challenge

The Palms Casino Resort in Las Vegas wanted new tools to drive additional hotel bookings. They'd been sending past guests a monthly promotional newsletter that generated revenue, but the executive team wanted a way to increase direct hotel bookings. They turned to us.

The Solution

We built a promotional engine the Palms could use to create custom games inside their newsletter — making communications far more interactive and giving their marketing team a powerful tool to design and deploy their own gamified promotions, with rewards like loyalty points and dining offers to redeem on the next visit.

The Result

In short, it worked. Patrons loved the games: hotel revenue from the newsletter jumped, open rates climbed, and click-throughs to the booking engine skyrocketed.

Financial Services · Modernization

Legacy Platform Re-Architecture

The Challenge

A large financial-services client's core platform ran on Adobe Flash. When Flash was sunset, another firm built a replacement that ignored modern best practices — even using the front-end as part of the transaction flow. The result: critical errors, unhappy customers, and lost accounts. Plan A was hired to re-architect the flawed sections while keeping it live in production.

The Solution

Our engineers mapped every component tied to the transaction flow, wrote automation tests to guarantee no lost functionality, then designed a new transaction engine using event sourcing, circuit-breaker patterns and more — and removed the front-end from the transactional workflow entirely.

The Result

Clear transaction states, scalable and robust execution, and dramatic improvements in uptime and errors. We stopped the transactional-engine emergencies, avoided a second expensive rebuild, met new regulatory requirements, and stopped the bleed of clients.

Healthcare · Product

Chronic Care Platform

The Challenge

A hospital asked us to build a patient communication and engagement platform from scratch for people with chronic conditions — an informal forum where patients connect with each other, with clinical moderators educating and motivating the group to follow their care plans and reduce costly readmissions.

The Solution

We built a secure, clinically-monitored forum for patients to ask questions, get support, and encourage one another. It closely tracks usage and can send "red flag alerts" to nurses and/or family members if a patient suddenly disengages or raises serious issues needing immediate intervention.

The Result

We've received glowing feedback from patients and the hospital. The project is in active development and continues to evolve — and we look forward to bringing tools like it to other hospitals, insurers, gyms and schools.

Blue Collar Environments · Platform

Field Dispatch Modernization

The Challenge

An audio-video installation company had built proprietary software to dispatch electricians and installers to jobs. It was promising but not built to modern standards — and the owners wanted to resell it to non-competitive verticals. They hired Plan A to modernize it and add multi-tenant functionality plus a long list of new features so it could be resold to lawn & pool care, plumbing, utilities and more.

The Solution

We re-architected and rebuilt the solution from scratch on .NET and React, with mobile apps in React Native. The new platform supports new verticals and has expanded quickly.

The Result

Far easier to use and packed with features — field staff can shoot videos, present proposals, collect payments and request help; a new analytics platform measures field efficiency; and jobs auto-route to the closest person. Ownership has resold it to multiple companies, generating more than 20x what they spent building it.

Government & Non-Profits · Civic

Tribal Citizen Portal

The Challenge

A tribe in the Pacific Northwest needed a citizen-engagement platform to cut the manual labor of keeping tribal citizens informed and engaged. They turned to Plan A to build the solution from scratch.

The Solution

We created a comprehensive citizen portal: citizens can vote on issues; apply for hunting and fishing licenses; register births, marriages and deaths; view healthcare information; apply for jobs; update contact info; apply for education-reimbursement credits; view government benefits; and much more.

The Result

Citizens are thrilled and looking forward to more functionality. The tribe was so pleased they're having us build a white-labeled version that can be sold to other tribal governments.
