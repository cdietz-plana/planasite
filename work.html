The Palms — Gamified Offers

Gaming, Entertainment & Hospitality

The Challenge

The Palms Casino Resort in Las Vegas wanted a new set of tools to drive additional hotel bookings to the property. They had been sending past guests a monthly email newsletter packed with promotions. The newsletter generated revenue, but the executive team wanted a way to increase direct hotel bookings. They turned to us.

The Solution

We built a promotional engine the Palms could use to build custom games to incorporate into their newsletter. This made the communications far more interactive and exciting, and gave their marketing team a powerful new marketing tool to actually design and deploy their own custom gamified promotions. As a result, gamified promotions were incorporated into their emails to past guests, and rewards like loyalty points and dining offers were given to patrons to redeem during their next visit.

The Result

In short, it worked. Patrons loved the games: Hotel revenues from the email newsletter jumped by $32,000 per month compared to the previous approach that didn't utilize our tools. Open rates on the email increased by 14%. Click-through rates to the booking engine skyrocketed by 103%.

Legacy Platform Re-Architecture

Financial Services

The Challenge

A large financial services client originally used Adobe Flash for its core platform. When Adobe announced it was sunsetting Flash, our client hired another company to create a new platform. Unfortunately, the other company built a profoundly flawed system that didn't follow any modern best practices or design patterns. One of the most egregious design flaws was the use of the front-end as part of the transaction flow. This led to a wide range of critical errors, many unhappy customers, and even lost client accounts. Plan A was hired to re-architect flawed sections of the platform while still keeping it operational in production.

The Solution

Our engineers identified all the components that needed to be modified based on the transaction flow. We created automation tests to ensure no functionality would be lost post-conversion. We then designed a new transaction engine using event sourcing, circuit breaker design patterns, and much more. Plus, of course, we removed the front-end from the transactional workflow.

The Result

This extremely challenging task generated deeply satisfying outcomes, including: Clear states of the transaction. Scalable and robust execution. Dramatic improvements in uptime and errors — we stopped emergencies related to the transactional engine. Avoided a second expensive rebuild. Made the solution compliant with new regulatory requirements. Stopped the bleed of clients. Enabled much faster uploads and downloads of key media. Launched new functionality.

Chronic Care Platform

Healthcare

The Challenge

A hospital approached us about developing a new patient communication and engagement platform from scratch for patients suffering from chronic conditions. They wanted an informal forum for patients to connect with each other, with clinical moderators helping educate and motivate the group to comply with their clinical instructions to reduce adverse events and expensive hospital readmissions.

The Solution

We collaborated with the hospital to create a secure, clinically-monitored forum for patients to ask questions, get support, and encourage other patients who ask to participate in the program. The platform closely tracks usage and has the ability to send out "red flag alerts" to nurses and/or family members if a patient either suddenly stops participating or raises serious issues that require immediate intervention.

The Result

We've already received glowing feedback from patients and the hospital. The project is still in active development and continues to evolve; we look forward to being able to bring tools like this to other hospitals, insurance companies, gyms, and schools.

Field Dispatch Modernization

Blue Collar Environments

The Challenge

This audio video installation company built their own proprietary software to dispatch electricians and installation staff to different jobs. Their product was promising, but it wasn't built using modern standards. What's more, the owners wanted to resell the software to other verticals that were non-competitive. They hired Plan A to modernize the dispatch software and add multi-tenant functionality, along with a long list of new features to allow the system to be resold to multiple new verticals that could also use dispatch software (such as lawn and pool care teams, plumbing companies, utility companies, and more).

The Solution

We re-architected and rebuilt the solution from scratch using .NET and React, with a set of mobile apps built using ReactNative. The new solution was able to support new verticals and has expanded quickly.

The Result

The new solution is far easier to use and boasts many more features, allowing staff in the field to take videos on their phones or tablets, present proposals to customers, collect payments, request additional help, and much more. A new analytics platform analyzes efficiency of employees in the field. The software automatically routes the closest person to the closest job, saving commuting time each day. Ownership has now been able to resell the solution to multiple other companies, generating more than 20x the amount of money that they spent with Plan A building the solution for themselves.

Tribal Citizen Portal

Government & Non-Profits

The Challenge

A tribe in the Pacific Northwest needed a citizen engagement platform to minimize the amount of manual labor being done to keep its tribal citizens informed and engaged. They turned to Plan A Technologies to build the solution from scratch.

The Solution

We created a comprehensive citizen engagement portal solution for them. The tool allows citizens to vote on issues; apply for hunting and fishing licenses; register births, marriages, and deaths; view healthcare information; apply for jobs; update contact information; apply for education reimbursement credits; view received government benefits; and much more.

The Result

The citizens are thrilled, and are looking forward to rolling out even more functionality in the near future. The tribe was so pleased that they're having us create a white-labeled version of the platform that can be sold to other tribal governments.
