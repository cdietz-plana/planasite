Case study · Chronic care

Chronic Care Management platform for a large hospital

01 Challenge02 Solution03 Result

The Challenge

A hospital approached us about developing a new patient communication and engagement platform from scratch for patients suffering from chronic conditions. They wanted an informal forum for patients to connect with each other, with clinical moderators helping educate and motivate the group to comply with their clinical instructions — reducing adverse events and expensive hospital readmissions.

The Solution

We collaborated with the hospital to create a secure, clinically-monitored forum for patients to ask questions, get support, and encourage others in the program. The platform closely tracks usage and can send "red flag alerts" to nurses and/or family members if a patient suddenly stops participating or raises serious issues that require immediate intervention, notably potential suicides.

The Result

We've already received glowing feedback from patients and the hospital. The project is still in active development and continues to evolve; we look forward to bringing tools like this to other hospitals, insurance companies, gyms, and schools.
