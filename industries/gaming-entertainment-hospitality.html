Case study · The Palms

Award-winning gamified offer management for the Palms

01 Challenge02 Solution03 Result

The Challenge

The Palms Casino Resort in Las Vegas wanted new tools to drive additional hotel bookings. They'd been sending past guests a monthly promotional newsletter that generated revenue, but the executive team wanted a way to increase direct hotel bookings. They turned to us.

The Solution

We built a promotional engine the Palms could use to create custom games inside their newsletter — making communications far more interactive and giving their marketing team a powerful tool to design and deploy their own gamified promotions, with rewards like loyalty points and dining offers to redeem on the next visit.

The Result

In short, it worked. Patrons loved the games: hotel revenue from the email newsletter jumped compared to the previous approach, open rates climbed, and click-throughs to the booking engine skyrocketed.
