Case study · Citizen portal

A custom citizen engagement portal for a Pacific Northwest tribe

01 Challenge02 Solution03 Result

The Challenge

A tribe in the Pacific Northwest needed a citizen engagement platform to minimize the amount of manual labor being done to keep its tribal citizens informed and engaged. They turned to Plan A Technologies to build the solution from scratch.

The Solution

We created a comprehensive citizen engagement portal for them. The tool allows citizens to vote on issues; apply for hunting and fishing licenses; register births, marriages, and deaths; view healthcare information; apply for jobs; update contact information; apply for education reimbursement credits; view received government benefits; and much more.

The Result

The citizens are thrilled, and are looking forward to rolling out even more functionality in the near future. The tribe was so pleased that they're having us create a white-labeled version of the platform that can be sold to other tribal governments.
