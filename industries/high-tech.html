Case study · SaaS scale-up

Scaled a SaaS platform for 10x growth

01 Challenge02 Solution03 Result

The Challenge

A fast-growing SaaS company was winning enterprise customers faster than their platform could handle. A single-tenant monolith was buckling under load — slow deploys, noisy-neighbor incidents, and cloud bills climbing faster than revenue. They needed to scale for a projected 10x in traffic without a risky rewrite or a stall in their product roadmap.

The Solution

We embedded architects and engineers alongside their team to move to a multi-tenant, service-oriented architecture on a modern cloud stack. We introduced autoscaling, caching, an event-driven pipeline and CI/CD with automated testing, and applied FinOps practices to right-size infrastructure — all shipped incrementally so the product kept moving the whole way.

The Result

The platform now comfortably absorbs traffic spikes an order of magnitude larger than before, with faster page loads and dramatically fewer incidents. Infrastructure spend dropped meaningfully even as usage grew, and the team ships new features with confidence. The engagement continues as they expand into new markets.
