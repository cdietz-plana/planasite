Scaled a SaaS Platform for 10x Growth

Case Study · illustrative

The Challenge

A fast-growing B2B SaaS company was signing enterprise customers faster than their platform could handle. Their monolithic architecture buckled under load, deploys were risky and slow, and their small engineering team was stuck fighting fires instead of building the features that were winning deals. They needed to scale — without a rewrite that would stall the roadmap.

The Solution

We embedded a dedicated team alongside theirs and incrementally decomposed the monolith into well-defined services, introduced a modern CI/CD pipeline with automated testing, and moved core workloads to an elastic cloud architecture. We added observability across the stack and shipped an AI-assisted feature set to differentiate the product — all while keeping the existing platform live for customers.

The Result

The platform now handles more than ten times its previous peak traffic with room to spare. Deploy frequency went from weekly and nerve-racking to many times a day and routine, and infrastructure spend per customer dropped meaningfully. Freed from firefighting, the client's team shipped their most ambitious roadmap yet.
